PeriProof privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: PeriProof has no account and no server. Everything you log stays on your phone. We never see it, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

What PeriProof stores

The symptoms, severity ratings, counts, times of day, bleeding, notes, medication and HRT changes you enter, your settings (such as cycle mode and chosen symptoms), the optional first name and birth year shown on your Doctor Card, and copies of Doctor Cards you have shared. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to share it. Patterns and the Doctor Card are worked out on the phone itself. When you tap "Share or save PDF", your phone's share menu opens and you decide where the PDF goes, for example email, WhatsApp or your files. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

PeriProof contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party sign-in. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchases

If you buy PeriProof Plus, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We receive no card details and no health data from the purchase.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can export a copy any time (Settings → Export my data) and delete everything (Settings → Delete all my data). Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover lost data.

Backups

PeriProof does not upload backups. A future optional, end-to-end encrypted backup will be off unless you turn it on, and this policy will be updated before it is offered.

Children

PeriProof is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Medical information

PeriProof is not a medical device and does not give a diagnosis or medical advice. Talk to a doctor, nurse or pharmacist about your symptoms and treatment.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights over your personal data. Because PeriProof holds none of your data on any server, you exercise them directly in the app (export and delete). For any question, contact us at lanansipsip@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the app's store listing will link to the new version.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email lanansipsip@gmail.com. Please don't send health details by email.
